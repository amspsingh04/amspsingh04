When we talk about the 2022 WC, and the insane final it showcased, it is quite impossible to ignore that Argentina were strong favourites for winning the tournament, on the back of Copa America 2021, Finalissima 2022 victories, and the 36 (ig?) game unbeaten run in the lead up to the world cup. They had been undefeated since before covid was a public issue, and had maintained that until after covid. So it did come as a very big shock when they were defeated by the weakest team in their group, Saudi Arabia. Now, if we talk about Asian footballing powerhouses, the usual suspects are Japan and South Korea, and to some extent Iran and Australia. Saudi Arabia has been a perennially underachieving team, especially in this century. So that match on Nov 22, 2022, did seem like an anomalous result from the POVs of both Saudi and Argentina Football teams, but that is just the eye test. Does the data also say the same thing? Let us check out what we can infer.
